# Support

`timeweb-rs` is free and open source. If it saved you time — a deployment
that went smoothly, an integration that did not fight back, an afternoon you
did not spend debugging a deserializer — you can say thanks.

> A donation is a thank-you, not a purchase. It buys no service and no
> priority; the issues and the pull requests are handled on a best-effort
> basis either way.

## What the support pays for

- The weekly regeneration of the client when the upstream specification
  changes.
- The investigation of the live differences between the specification and the
  API: the client stops failing on the responses the vendor already sends.
- The time to answer the issues and to review the pull requests.

## Other ways to help

- Open an issue or a pull request.
- Tell someone about the SDK.
