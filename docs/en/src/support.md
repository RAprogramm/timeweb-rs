# Support

`timeweb-rs` is free and open source, and it is maintained in the open: the
issues, the pull requests and the weekly regeneration of the client are all
public. If it saves you time, you can support the work.

> A donation is a gratuitous transfer, not a purchase of software, and it
> does not buy any service or priority. Issues and pull requests are handled
> on a best-effort basis regardless of the support.

when the upstream specification changes.
