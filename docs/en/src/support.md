# Support

`timeweb-rs` is free and open source, and it is maintained in the open: the
issues, the pull requests and the weekly regeneration of the client are all
public. If it saves you time, you can support the work.

> A donation is a gratuitous transfer, not a purchase of software, and it
> does not buy any service or priority. Issues and pull requests are handled
> on a best-effort basis regardless of the support.

> **A note for Russian residents.** A crypto donation is income, and since
> 2025 the tax on the professional income regime (НПД) does not apply to the
> acquisition or the disposal of digital currency — converting the donation
> into rubles is such a disposal. The tax treatment therefore depends on your
> status: the personal income tax of 13–15% with a 3-НДФЛ declaration, or
> the simplified taxation of an individual entrepreneur at 6%. Inside Russia,
> crypto cannot be accepted as a payment for works and services since
> September 2026. If you are a Russian resident, use the Russian version of
> this page — the link in the header switches the language. The tax treatment
> of your donation is yours to assess.

## What the support pays for

- The weekly regeneration of the client when the upstream specification
  changes.
- The investigation of the live differences between the specification and the
  API: the client stops failing on the responses the vendor already sends.
- The time to answer the issues and to review the pull requests.
